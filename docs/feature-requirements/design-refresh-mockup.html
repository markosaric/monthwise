What the drawing shows

  • Left menu (FR-6 to FR-8): one toggle button collapses the menu to coloured icons and expands it again. System status appears at the bottom only on a problem (use the buttons above the drawing).
  • Dashboard (FR-9 to FR-11): four key figures with the change against last month, a highlight card with the next due payment, Coming up for the next 7 days, the month result with a 6-month chart, outputs by kind, and small cards for Loans, Card payments, Shared costs and Recurring bills. No balances and no list of cards.
  • Cards (FR-12): select Cards in the menu. The accounts with current and expected balances, status and the starting balance hint moved here from the Dashboard, with totals and a balance chart, followed by the cards with installments.
  • Quick actions (FR-14): coloured icon buttons at the top of the Dashboard; rest the pointer on one for about a second to see its label. On Month the same buttons stay inside their sections.
  • Month, Cash flow, Loans, Shared costs, Settings (FR-15 to FR-20): select them in the menu. Savings is not drawn. All amounts are made up.
